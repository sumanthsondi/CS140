About Borra Caves

Borra Caves, also called Borra Guhalu, are located in the Ananthagiri Hills near Araku Valley in Andhra Pradesh. They are famous for their impressive natural rock formations.

Inside the caves, visitors can see interesting formations created naturally over thousands of years. Borra Caves are a popular attraction for visitors interested in nature, geology, and adventure.
