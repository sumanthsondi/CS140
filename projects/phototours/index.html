Welcome to Vizag

Visakhapatnam, also known as Vizag, is a beautiful coastal city in Andhra Pradesh, India. It is often called the "City of Destiny." Vizag is known for its beaches, hills, valleys, caves, and beautiful natural scenery.

This photo tour introduces five beautiful places in and around Vizag. Each location has its own unique scenery and attractions. Explore the pages below to learn more about these places.

Places on This Photo Tour

  • Araku Valley - A beautiful valley surrounded by green hills.
  • Kailasagiri - A hilltop attraction with beautiful views of Vizag.
  • Vanajangi - A scenic mountain destination famous for clouds and sunrise views.
  • Rushikonda Beach - A beautiful beach along the Bay of Bengal.
  • Borra Caves - Natural caves with fascinating rock formations.

About the Tour

From the green mountains of Araku Valley to the beaches of Rushikonda, Vizag has many places to explore. Borra Caves offer a different experience with their natural underground formations. Kailasagiri provides beautiful views of the city, while Vanajangi is known for its peaceful mountain scenery.
