My Hero: Tom Holland

Hero Interests | Profile

Tom Holland is an actor who is well known for playing Spider-Man in Marvel movies. I like him because he brings humor and energy to the Spider-Man character. His Spider-Man movies are some of the movies I enjoy watching the most.

Spider-Man: Homecoming
Tom Holland played Peter Parker, also known as Spider-Man.
Spider-Man: Far From Home
Tom Holland returned as Peter Parker and continued his story as Spider-Man.
Spider-Man: No Way Home
Tom Holland played Peter Parker again and worked with other Spider-Man characters.
