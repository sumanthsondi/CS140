1. What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

YouTube makes it easy to upload, share, and stream videos. It also saves website storage space. However, advertisements may appear, and I have less control over the video.

2. What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

I can move, rotate, scale, or change the position of an element without moving other elements around it.

3. What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes an element's position, size, or rotation. A transition makes a change happen smoothly over time.
