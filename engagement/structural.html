What goes in the header?

The header contains introductory information such as the website title, logo, heading, or navigation menu.

What goes in the nav?

The nav contains important navigation links that help users move around a website.

Can the nav go inside the header?

Yes, the nav can be placed inside the header.

Does the nav have to be inside the header?

No, the nav can be inside the header or placed separately.

Does the nav contain links to another website?

Not necessarily. It usually contains links to pages or sections of your own website, but it can also contain links to other websites.

What goes in main?

The main contains the main and most important content of the webpage.

What goes in the footer?

The footer contains information at the bottom of the webpage, such as copyright information, author information, or additional links.

HTML validation?

HTML validation is checking your HTML code to make sure it follows HTML rules and does not have errors.
