1. What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start on a new line and normally take up the available width of their container. Inline tags stay within the same line and only take up as much space as their content needs.

2. What are margin, border, and padding?

Padding is the space between the content and the border. The border surrounds the content and padding. Margin is the space outside the border that separates the box from other elements.

3. What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

CSS units include pixels (px), percentages (%), em, rem, viewport width (vw), viewport height (vh), points (pt), inches (in), centimeters (cm), and millimeters (mm).

4. When would you use a figure tag?

I would use a figure tag when I want to give an image, diagram, or other content its own block and include a caption that describes it.
